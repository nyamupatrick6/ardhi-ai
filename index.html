FAQs

Frequently asked questions

How long does a boundary survey take?

Many boundary surveys can be completed on site within a day, with final documentation depending on plot size, terrain, location and the scope of work.

What documents do I need for a title or registry search?

Typically the title deed or land reference number and the names of the registered owner. The exact requirements depend on the matter.

How much does a land survey cost?

Pricing depends on the service, plot size, location and scope. The price list on this site provides starting figures for selected services.

How do I know if a title deed is genuine?

A website screening tool cannot authenticate a title. Proper verification requires authoritative registry evidence, professional survey evidence and, where appropriate, legal review.

Do you work outside Nairobi?

Yes. Ardhi AI is positioned to connect clients with professional land services across Kenya, subject to location and scope.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 August 2026

Ardhi AI, operated by Benchmark Land Surveying (“Ardhi AI”, “we”, “us” or “our”), is committed to protecting personal data and the constitutional right to privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, retain and protect personal data when you use the Ardhi AI website, Land Intelligence Check™, Virtual Land Assistant, document workspace, callback tools, WhatsApp/email contact channels and related professional services.

Important legal notice: This Policy is intended to reflect the Kenyan data-protection framework applicable to Ardhi AI, including the Constitution of Kenya, the Data Protection Act, 2019 and the Data Protection Regulations, 2021. Publication of this Policy alone does not constitute a certification of legal compliance. Ardhi AI must maintain the required organisational, technical, contractual, registration and governance measures applicable to its actual processing activities.

1. Legal and regulatory framework

Ardhi AI's privacy practices are designed with reference to:

  • Article 31 of the Constitution of Kenya, including the right to privacy;
  • The Data Protection Act, 2019 (including its principles, lawful bases, data-subject rights, security, retention, transfers and breach obligations);
  • The Data Protection (General) Regulations, 2021;
  • The Data Protection (Registration of Data Controllers and Data Processors) Regulations, 2021, where applicable;
  • The Data Protection (Complaints Handling and Enforcement) Regulations, 2021; and
  • Other applicable Kenyan laws, professional obligations, contractual requirements and regulatory directions that may apply to a particular land, surveying, mapping or technology service.

2. Who is responsible for your personal data?

Depending on the particular service and processing activity, Ardhi AI may act as a data controller, a data processor, or another party acting under documented instructions. The applicable role depends on who determines the purpose and means of processing. Where Ardhi AI processes information on behalf of a client or professional organisation, that relationship may be governed by a separate written data-processing arrangement.

3. Personal data we may collect

Depending on what you ask us to do, we may collect only information reasonably necessary for the stated purpose, including:

  • Identity/contact data: name, telephone number, email address and preferred contact channel;
  • Property and land-matter information: parcel/LR/title references, county, locality, transaction purpose and information supplied during a land enquiry;
  • Document information: information contained in title documents, searches, mutations, RIM sheets, survey plans, leases, correspondence and other supporting documents that you choose to submit;
  • Spatial information: coordinates, maps, boundary information, survey observations and related geospatial evidence where required for a service;
  • Communication records: enquiries, messages, service requests, callback selections and correspondence;
  • Technical information: device/browser information, IP address, timestamps, diagnostic information and similar information generated when the website is used, where collected by the site's infrastructure or service providers; and
  • Uploaded files: files and their metadata where you voluntarily submit documents through a document workspace or another supported channel.

Please do not upload or disclose another person's personal information unless you are authorised to do so or another lawful basis permits the disclosure. If a document contains unnecessary personal information, redact it where reasonably possible before submission.

4. Data minimisation and purpose limitation

Ardhi AI will seek to collect information that is adequate, relevant and limited to what is necessary for the purpose for which it is processed. We will not intentionally use personal data for an incompatible purpose merely because it is available to us.

Examples of purposes include: responding to enquiries; arranging consultations; providing land, surveying, GIS or geospatial services; preparing or communicating professional deliverables; processing a Land Intelligence Check™; receiving and organising documents; following up a service request; maintaining security and audit records; complying with legal obligations; preventing misuse; and handling complaints or legal claims.

5. Lawful bases for processing

Where required by law, Ardhi AI will identify and rely on an applicable lawful basis for processing. Depending on the activity, this may include:

  • Consent — where you give valid, specific and informed consent;
  • Contract / pre-contract steps — where processing is necessary to provide a requested service or take steps at your request;
  • Legal obligation — where processing is required by law or a lawful regulatory requirement;
  • Legitimate interests — where legally available and where those interests are not overridden by your rights and freedoms;
  • Vital interests / public interest — only where the relevant legal conditions apply.

Consent is not assumed merely because you visit the website. Where consent is the applicable basis, you will be given appropriate information and a meaningful opportunity to provide or withdraw consent, subject to lawful exceptions and consequences of withdrawal.

6. Land documents and property information

Land documents can contain personal, financial, ownership, family, signature, identification and other sensitive information. Ardhi AI treats documents supplied for a land matter as confidential business/professional information and applies appropriate safeguards to the extent required by the applicable law and the actual processing arrangement.

Uploading a document does not by itself authenticate the document. The Land Intelligence Check™ and document-intelligence features are screening and information-organisation tools. They do not replace an official registry search, title authentication, licensed survey, valuation, statutory approval, advocate's legal opinion or other professional verification where one is required.

7. Virtual Land Assistant and artificial intelligence

The Virtual Land Assistant may process information you enter into the conversation so that it can respond, guide you toward an appropriate service and identify when professional or human intervention is required.

  • AI responses are informational and are not automatically a legal opinion, statutory decision, title authentication or survey determination.
  • Where a matter requires professional judgement or official verification, Ardhi AI may direct the matter to an appropriate human/professional workflow.
  • Ardhi AI will not represent an AI-generated screening result as an official government record.
  • Where automated processing is used in a manner that is legally subject to safeguards concerning significant decisions, applicable data-subject rights and human-review mechanisms will be respected.

8. Document workspace and verification requests

If you select documents for verification or professional follow-up, Ardhi AI may use the information supplied to organise the matter, identify apparent inconsistencies, prepare a preliminary workflow, communicate with you and connect the matter to the requested professional service.

If you choose Send via Email or Send via WhatsApp, the selected information may leave the Ardhi AI website and be transmitted through the service you selected. The privacy and security practices of those third-party communication providers also apply.

When you use WhatsApp, email, mapping services, hosting services, analytics tools or other third-party infrastructure, information may be processed by those providers under their own terms and privacy practices. Ardhi AI will seek to use appropriate contractual, technical or other safeguards where required by applicable law.

You should review the privacy terms of a third-party provider before sending documents or personal data through that provider, particularly where the material contains identity, ownership, financial, family or other confidential information.

10. Sharing and disclosure

Ardhi AI does not sell personal data merely because you use the website. Personal data may be disclosed where necessary and lawful to:

  • professionals or service providers engaged to perform the requested land, survey, GIS or related service;
  • data processors and technology providers acting under appropriate instructions and safeguards;
  • government bodies, registries, regulators or other authorities where disclosure is required or permitted by law or necessary for the requested professional process;
  • legal, audit, compliance or professional advisers where reasonably necessary;
  • courts, law-enforcement agencies or other competent authorities where legally required; or
  • another party where you have given the required consent or otherwise authorised the disclosure.

We seek to limit disclosure to information reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose.

Depending on the system and processing involved, safeguards may include access controls, authentication, confidentiality obligations, secure transmission, restricted permissions, logging, backup controls, secure disposal and incident-response procedures.

No internet transmission, email service, messaging service or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely risk-free. You should therefore avoid submitting information that is unnecessary for the requested service.
