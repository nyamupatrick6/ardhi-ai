Common questions

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before verifying your land or booking a survey.

How do I verify land in Kenya before buying?+

Start with a registry search to confirm the title is genuine and free of disputes, then commission a boundary survey to confirm the physical plot matches the title. Ardhi AI runs both, cross-checking the title, boundary, and registry record against each other before you send any money.

How long does a boundary survey take?+

Most boundary surveys are completed on-site within a day, with the final report delivered shortly after, depending on plot size, terrain, and location. You'll get a firm timeline once you request a quote.

What documents do I need for a title or registry search?+

Typically the title deed or land reference number, and the names of the registered owner. If you're unsure what you have, send a photo of your documents on WhatsApp and we'll confirm what's needed.

How much does a land survey cost?+

Pricing depends on the service — registry searches and beacon relocations start from a few thousand shillings, while boundary and topographic surveys are priced by plot size and terrain. Full pricing is listed in the services section above, or message us for a firm quote.

How do I know if a title deed is genuine or fraudulent?+

A genuine title should match the physical boundary on the ground and have a clean registry record with no competing claims. Our registry search and AI-cross-verified reports flag mismatches between the title, boundary, and registry — the most common signs of land fraud in Kenya.

Do you work outside Nairobi?+

Yes — our surveyors and fieldwork teams take on jobs across Kenya. Message us your plot's location and we'll confirm scheduling and any travel costs upfront.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Ardhi AI, operated by Benchmark Land Surveying ("Ardhi AI", "we", "us"), respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data in accordance with Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019. This policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, and how we handle it.

1. Information We Collect

When you contact us through our website, WhatsApp, phone, or callback request form, we may collect:

  • Your name
  • Your phone number
  • Details of the survey or land-related service you're enquiring about
  • Any additional information you choose to share with us during correspondence

We also use GoatCounter, a privacy-focused analytics tool, to understand general site traffic patterns. It does not use cookies or track individual visitors across other websites.

2. Why We Collect It

We use your information solely to:

  • Respond to your enquiry or callback request
  • Provide a quote for surveying or geospatial services
  • Communicate with you about a survey you've requested or are undertaking with us

3. How We Store and Protect It

Your contact details are stored securely and are accessible only to Ardhi AI / Benchmark Land Surveying staff who need them to respond to your enquiry. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties.

4. WhatsApp Communication

If you contact us via WhatsApp, that conversation is subject to WhatsApp's own privacy practices in addition to this policy. We use WhatsApp only to communicate directly with you about your enquiry.

5. Your Rights

Under the Data Protection Act, 2019, you have the right to:

  • Know what personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data, where applicable
  • Object to or restrict certain uses of your data
  • Lodge a complaint with the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC), Kenya

6. Data Retention

We retain your contact information only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purpose it was collected for, or as required by law.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect the most recent revision.
